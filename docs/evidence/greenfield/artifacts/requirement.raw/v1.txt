We need a URL shortener service for customer communications.
- Create a short link for a long URL, optionally with a custom alias and an expiry time.
- Visiting a short link redirects to the original URL. Expired or deleted links must stop working.
- Look up a link's details, and delete a link.
- The service will be on the public internet: validate input, make it hard to abuse for
  phishing (e.g. credentials-in-URL tricks, redirect loops) and rate-limit link creation.
- Errors must be consistent and machine-readable. Publish an OpenAPI contract and docs.
- Persist links in PostgreSQL.
